CONTROLS

ON A PHONE OR TABLET

Left stickDrag down = nose up, up = nose down, sideways = roll / steer
THR sliderSlide up for more power, down for less
APAutopilot: flies to the destination and lands (move the stick or throttle to take over)
GEAR / FLAPS / BRAKELanding gear, flaps (up → 1 → full), wheel brakes (hold)
CAM / DAY/NIGHT / IIChange camera, day or night, pause menu
Drag the viewLook around in the Orbit camera

FLY YOURSELF

SHIFT / ZThrottle up / down (hold)
S / W or ↓ / ↑Nose up / nose down (I inverts)
A / D or ← / →Roll left / right (steer on the ground)
Q / ERudder left / right
GLanding gear up / down
VFlaps: up → 1 → full
B or SPACEWheel brakes (hold)
C or 1-5Camera: Chase, Cockpit, Orbit (drag), Tower, Map
PAutopilot on / off: flies to the destination and lands automatically (any flight key takes over)
TFlight assist on / off (off = full aerobatics)
RRestart the flight
MMute engine sound
